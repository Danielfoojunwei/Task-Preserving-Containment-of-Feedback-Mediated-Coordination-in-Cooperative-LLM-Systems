# Mechanism map after the sealed main study and fixed continuations

Prepared after the coordinator's numerical release on 16 October 2026. This is
an interpretation supplement, not a change to either frozen experimental plan.
It extends the older observation map without modifying that frozen file.

## The supported connection

Removing a communication opportunity did not simply make this benchmark safer.
From fresh task histories, withholding delivered notes increased late-round
bilateral reward under public auditing and a real simulated penalty. Once agents
had accumulated two rounds of observed history, withdrawing future notes left
accurate reimbursement intact; removing the entire prior actor context instead
restored unsupported payments despite allowing current notes.

These observations connect communication, history, and enforcement. They do not
identify a single psychological mediator, demonstrate covert agreement, or show
that every communication restriction backfires. Trusted receipt enforcement
provides a separate, inspectable payment invariant regardless of the content of
messages or the semantic accuracy of an agreement detector.

## What the continuation cohort represents

The continuation experiment includes all20 source worlds from the
notes-on/public-audit/fine12 arm at round2. It did not select20 formed coalitions.
Most source worlds had no accepted harmful pact. It tests persistence and
intervention from these observed histories, and cannot be described as20
established collusive coalitions dismantled.

The main primary family consists of four tests only. The continuation plan is
descriptive and supplies no additional primary p-values or confidence intervals.
Main summary tables that pool rounds1–4 must not be substituted for the primary
rounds2–4 metric. All game rewards, losses, and fines are simulated benchmark
units; API spending is separately measured in USD.

## Minimal follow-up that would separate the remaining mechanisms

A receipt-only relay, compared with policy-only reminders, ordinary notes, and
no delivery, would distinguish task-relevant information from richer negotiation
content. Separate removals of notes, actor memory, public report history and
financial history would identify which retained context sustains accurate
approvals. These were not run in the present cohorts. They should be frozen as
new tests rather than inferred from an actor's generated private plan.
