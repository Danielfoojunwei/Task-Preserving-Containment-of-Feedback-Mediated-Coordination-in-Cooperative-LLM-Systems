# Control-plane research release — 15 October 2026

Two new live studies are complete: **2,326 research calls**, plus six compatibility requests, using OpenRouter `openai/gpt-4.1-mini` and `openai/gpt-4.1`, temperature 0.7, maximum 1,500 response tokens. Stored provider usage totals 2,170,126 tokens and $1.8537608. These are response-reported costs, not a reconciled account invoice. No key is included.

The [manuscript](controlplane_20261015/PAPER_CONTROLPLANE.md) reports the original nulls, measurement audit and separate follow-up. The [follow-up figure](hostmask_20261015/host_masked_composition.png) shows the key results and limitations together.

The supported contribution is a **controlled causal benchmark connecting joint host-generated metadata, actual model inference, accepted harmful decisions and simple-task utility**. XOR composition, control-flow leakage and noninterference are known principles. This is not a new information-theoretic discovery, evidence of spontaneous collusive intent, a full reproduction of the referenced systems, or a demonstrated break of their original implementations. See the [prior-art audit](controlplane_20261015/LITERATURE_POSITIONING.md) and [claim boundaries](controlplane_20261015/CLAIM_BOUNDARIES.md).
