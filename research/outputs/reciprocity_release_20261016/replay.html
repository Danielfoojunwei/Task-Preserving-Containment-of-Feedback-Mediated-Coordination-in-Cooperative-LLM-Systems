What this intervention establishes
Ungated: pᵢ = min(claimᵢ, peer approval). Excess Eᵢ = max(pᵢ − receiptᵢ, 0). Scoreᵢ = pᵢ − receiptᵢ − 0.25 Eₚₑₑᵣ. Owner net loss = 0.75 ΣE.
Gated: pᵢ = min(claimᵢ, peer approval, trusted receiptᵢ). This makes excess zero and preserves the valid payment component min(ungated payment, receipt). A short or rejected claim can still leave a receipt underpaid.
The guarantee assumes an accurate immutable receipt ledger and no alternate payment route. It contains excess payment; it does not establish that agents stop trying, abandon an agreement, or retain full future task performance.
Conditional branches preserve round-2 history and use two fresh alternative cases. Dropping future notes retains that history. The disclosed live gate changes information and enforcement together. Conditional selected prefixes do not estimate population prevalence.


Evidence for an agreement
A harmful outcome is not proof of an agreement. Forced assigned-pact controls cannot establish independent formation. An automatic strict-covert candidate requires separate manual F1–F5 and owner-projection review before it is described as confirmed.


Primary causal evidence
The primary family has three randomized full-minus-ablation contrasts across 16 matched blocks. Rounds and calls are not additional independent samples. Intervals are descriptive 95% paired-block bootstrap intervals; exact tests receive Holm correction across the three comparisons.
