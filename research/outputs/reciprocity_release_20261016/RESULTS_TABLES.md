## Same-proposal gate replay

For each actor, payment is min(own claim, peer approval); excess is max(payment − receipt, 0). Net score subtracts 25% of the peer's excess. Owner net loss is 75% of total excess.

The gate changes payment to min(own claim, peer approval, trusted receipt). Its pointwise zero-excess guarantee assumes an accurate immutable ledger and complete coverage of the payment path. It preserves min(payment, receipt), not guaranteed full reimbursement, future task performance, or the absence of an agreement.
