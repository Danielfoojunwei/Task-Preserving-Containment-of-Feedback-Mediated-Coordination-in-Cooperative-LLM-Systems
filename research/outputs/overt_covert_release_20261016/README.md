# Completed consequential-oversight evidence release

16 October 2026. Start with the [integrated working report](REPORT.md),
[five-slide explanation](../../../presentations/overt_covert_20261016/SLIDES.md),
[recorded-action replay](evidence_replay.html), [main tables](MAIN_RESULTS.csv),
[four primary contrasts](PRIMARY_CONTRASTS.csv), and
[source-linked illustrative cases](CASE_STUDIES.json).

The main study completed 6,608 calls including controls; all-source continuations
completed 1,600 fresh calls. Actors were GPT-4.1; operational auditors and
research annotators were GPT-4.1 mini, all at temperature0.7. Main and
continuation API costs were US$25.8435684 and US$7.5000316. Including the retained
halted-launch liabilities and neutral diagnostic, this extension accounted for
US$33.4256496. Older studies are separate, not silently pooled into these counts.

The supported primary effect is increased normalized retained bilateral benefit
when peer notes were withheld under public monitoring and fines (20 matched
blocks; exact p=0.00390625; Holm across four tests=0.015625). Of 80 messaging
worlds, five had confirmed executed overt harmful agreements, zero had confirmed
accepted agent-originated concealment, and one concealment label remained
unclear. A host-directed hidden control demonstrates a different capability.
Stopping future notes preserved zero excess in all20 continued source histories;
removing their prior interaction-context bundle produced excess in14 sources.
The current live gate comparison has a zero-loss floor; the earlier harmful
cohort and pointwise certificate provide separate defense evidence.

Every claim is bounded to this simulated, deliberately misaligned approval task.
Assistant review is not human gold standard. Full forensic agreement evidence
differs from a narrow monitor's available evidence. Receipt caps assume trusted
correct receipts and complete payment mediation; they do not prove that
collusion ends or sorting remains correct. See the report's limitations and
dual-use section. All work is pre-sprint. The official template, authors,
affiliations and the team's own final report prose remain a separate submission
task; this working PDF does not claim to fulfill it.
