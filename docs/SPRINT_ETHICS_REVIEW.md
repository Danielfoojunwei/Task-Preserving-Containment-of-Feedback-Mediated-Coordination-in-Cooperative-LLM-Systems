# Prompts for the required ethics and limitations appendix

AI-assisted review notes for the authors, not a submission-ready appendix. The Guidelines require a **Limitations and Dual-Use / Ethical Considerations** appendix. The team should write it in its own words and verify every claim against the actual final experiment.

## What the result establishes

- Which observations were registered, who could see them, and which actions actually executed?
- Does the evidence show a designed channel, live inference, accepted harmful decisions, coalition advantage or deliberate coordination? State only the demonstrated levels.
- Which findings are prior work, which are event-period increments, and which remain proposed?

## Limits that matter here

- The supported prior source copies a bit without an adversarial objective; the receiver has a supplied decoder. Mutual collusive intent and emergent strategy are unproven.
- Individual profitable attacks do not establish reliably positive average coalition profit; the prior mean-payoff interval crosses zero.
- Public-copy success does not establish preservation of realistic workflow utility.
- Finite transcript equality excludes unregistered timing, billing, caches, tool effects and compromised hosts.
- Synthetic stress baselines are not implementations of the original named information-flow defenses. No deployed-system vulnerability was established.
- Small model/world banks, provider stochasticity, invalidity, strategy selection and multiplicity constrain generalization.

## How the work could be misused

- Could exact prompts, codebooks or metadata examples make it easier to hide coordination from a text-only monitor?
- Could the demonstrations guide use of tool order, receipts or policy outcomes as covert signals?
- Could an audit score be used misleadingly to accuse innocently correlated agents, or to claim safety outside its observer scope?
- Could suppressing all warnings to hide a channel remove necessary information for legitimate work?

Explain the plausible misuse path without turning the appendix into deployment instructions. Connect risks to the actual released artifact rather than generic statements.

## Safeguards and facts to verify

- The existing studies use synthetic tasks and bits; verify that the final event experiment also avoids real market manipulation, real private records and harm to third parties before saying so.
- Pair channel examples with the tested mitigation, uncertainty and scope limits. Avoid universal prevention claims.
- Before public release, verify artifact contents for credentials and private information. The existing bundle's literal-credential scan is evidence about that check, not a proof against every kind of sensitive content.
- State actual model/API use, human and AI contributions, applicable permissions and costs accurately. Do not substitute inferred authorship or affiliations.
- Preserve audit failures and null results. A clear evidence boundary is part of reducing misuse and misleading safety claims.
