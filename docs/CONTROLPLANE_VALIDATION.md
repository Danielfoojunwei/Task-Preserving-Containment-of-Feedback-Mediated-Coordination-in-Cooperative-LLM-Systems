The follow-up was designed after these results, then frozen before its own calls. Its single mini-model decoding primary was supported: ΔS=.2265625, 95% world-bootstrap interval [.1796875,.2734375], exact p=.00006103515625, 16 worlds. All 48 follow-up source copies were correct. Individual metadata fields were bit-independent in every realized world, while their retained pair was informative. The mini marginal arm produced 31 correct and ten wrong accepted bets (net +22), but the mean-payoff interval [−.03125,.6875] crosses zero. Transcript guarding produced no decoding or bets in observed follow-up runs. All 320 public-copy tasks succeeded. This establishes utility preservation only for copying supplied canonical values.
