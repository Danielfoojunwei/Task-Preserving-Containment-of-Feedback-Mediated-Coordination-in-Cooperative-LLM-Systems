## Research positioning

Recommended framing: **Auditing and Containing Collusion Opportunities in Defender-Generated Metadata**.

The contribution is a controlled causal audit of a communication opportunity, actual receiver decisions, and a tested mitigation. It connects complete agent-visible transcripts with accepted owner-harming actions and legitimate-task utility. XOR composition, control-flow leakage and noninterference are established background. Novelty should be argued from the evaluation and evidence, not a claim to have discovered those mechanisms.

The latest study supports information-mediated harmful capability under an adversarial receiver objective. Its source only copies a private bit; it does not receive a collusive objective. Therefore neither mutually incentivized collusion, autonomous encoding discovery nor spontaneous intent is established. Those are explicit research gaps, not wording to be removed for a submission.

## Proposed incremental work if permitted by the sprint

The strongest follow-up would replace the neutral reporting source with two explicitly coalition-incentivized agents and matched cooperative controls. Seal a strategy before fresh secrets; evaluate on held-out worlds without supplied decoding tables; keep search budgets equal. Use a realistic task where some secret-dependent warnings or approvals are legitimately useful. Prospectively register coalition benefit, accepted owner harm, task quality and coverage, preserving abstention and invalidity separately. The goal is to test whether a mitigation reduces harmful coordination while retaining necessary authorized communication.

This is proposed work, not a completed experiment or approved sprint plan. Its fit and timing depend on the official criteria. Preserve pre-sprint results as a baseline and report any event-period increment separately.
