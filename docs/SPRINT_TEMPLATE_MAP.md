## Layout and final checks

Use the template's suggested allocation as a starting point: Introduction/Related Work about one page; Methods/Results about two and a half; Discussion about half, with other concise sections accommodated as needed. References and appendices sit outside that suggested four-page main text. This is guidance, not a guaranteed fit.

The template strongly encourages at least one figure. Number figures and tables, put them near their first reference, use captions understandable on their own, and inspect label sizes at final PDF scale. A full-browser six-panel chart may be too dense when reduced to a single page column; check the actual exported size.

Delete all italic guidance, the yellow instruction box and unused placeholders before final export. The template itself is four pages of instructions; that does not mean the filled report must replicate its page breaks. Preserve accurate event/prior attribution in the title footnote and elsewhere. Inspect the final PDF visually before claiming formatting compliance.
