## 1. Why this question matters, and what prior work leaves open

Markets and workplace studies already show that agents can benefit from coordination. We ask: **can a hidden plan become profitable harmful execution, and can settlement contain it while paying valid work?** We found no verified named deployed incident and make no production-system claim.

## 5. One connected contribution, scope and practical implication

**In this workflow, a hidden plan can execute through delegated payment authority; a trusted receipt boundary can contain the transfers even while the requests continue.** Reporting, agreement and execution are separate properties. Omitting agreement words can still leak a harmful action rule; material disclosures matter.

The contribution is the matched live result and source-supported conditional witness, not a new audit framework or theorem. Trusted receipts and complete mediation are assumptions.
