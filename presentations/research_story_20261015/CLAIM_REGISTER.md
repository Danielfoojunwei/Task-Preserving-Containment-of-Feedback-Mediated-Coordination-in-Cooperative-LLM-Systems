## Presentation safeguards

- Use **supported** only for a prespecified effect meeting its own registered rule; label post hoc sensitivities and crossed replay **exploratory**.
- Keep native Jira regret, synthetic resource harm and signed decoding on separate numerical axes or table columns. Do not normalize or rank them without a defensible common scale.
- Place main and follow-up decoding estimates in separately labeled study panels; the latter is a redesign, not a repaired original hypothesis test.
- Never describe the perfect copy-task performance as general task preservation, or no guarded bets as universal prevention.
- Do not put “first ever,” “proved collusion,” “bulletproof,” “no loopholes,” “deployed incident,” or “full four-paper reproduction” in narration.
- All results dated 15 October predate the 23–25 October sprint. A submission must disclose prior work and separately identify any actual sprint-period contribution.
