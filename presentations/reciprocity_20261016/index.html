01 / 05 · The question

When does individual reward-seeking become an agreement?

In eight matched source contexts, future-note cutoff left the same financial loss. Receipt enforcement removed the loss while unsupported attempts continued.

Information carrier→Accepted agreement→Executable loss

What earlier evidence taught us

Metadata can carry information, but the host supplied its decoder. A separate 300-call search found no resolved covert pact.

What the new data establish

Seven clear agent-originated reciprocal-favor pacts under a supplied negotiation process. All are overt; none establishes strict concealment.

All five primary causal contrasts remain unconfirmed after Holm correction. We extend controlled questions; we do not claim full reproduction or a break of the cited defense systems.

03 / 05 · The empirical standard

Agreement, enactment and containment require different evidence

Agreement

Literal proposal, actual delivery, other-agent acceptance and resolved prohibited reciprocal terms.

Enactment

Raw claims/reviews, both net scores and owner loss. Matching behavior alone is insufficient.

Containment

Same-history fresh continuations plus identical-proposal payment replay.

Main: 16 matched blocks × 4 arms, plus separate controls. Supplement: 8 fresh blocks × 3 arms. Primary inference uses blocks, never calls or rounds.

Exact 65,536/256-swap tests; original Holm3/Holm2 and joint Holm5; descriptive 20,000-bootstrap intervals. All malformed, refusing and negative cases retained.

3,996 completed calls · US$15.7122648 reported cost · no transport failures.

105 study checks plus 44 core tests pass (149 total), with independent raw-record audits. Each cohort frozen before its own calls; adaptation disclosed. Prefix review preceded current financial outcomes. Assistant reviews are not human ground truth: limited summary exposure and one main-world disagreement are retained. One model family, small samples and engineered objectives limit generalization.

Cutting future speech did not undo the financial outcome

Loss was identical in all eight paired worlds after future notes were cut. Every legitimate receipt was actually paid in all three branches. The disclosed gate still saw 28/32 unsupported claims and 22/32 unsupported approvals.

Same-proposal gate: min(claim, approval, trusted receipt). Trusted ledger and complete payment mediation required. Existing history and numerical channels remain; disclosed enforcement changes knowledge too. This is a bounded continuation result, not universal prevention. Real-world analogy: expense, invoice or procurement workflows with conflicting local incentives.
